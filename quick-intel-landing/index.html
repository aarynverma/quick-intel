How it works

Three clicks. One sharp summary.

No accounts. No sign-up. Just you, your API key, and the page you're reading.

1

Click the icon

Hit the Quick Intel bolt in your Chrome toolbar on any article, blog post, or documentation page.

2

Pick your style

Choose TL;DR for a quick take, bullet points for a scannable list, or detailed for a full breakdown.

3

Read the gist

Your AI provider returns a sharp, actionable summary in seconds. Copy it or browse your history anytime.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Three summary styles

TL;DR for quick skimming, bullet points for structured takeaways, or a multi-paragraph breakdown for deep reading.

Summary history

Your last 50 summaries are saved locally and always one click away. Browse, copy, or reopen the original page.

One-click copy

Copy any summary to your clipboard instantly with visual confirmation. Paste it into notes, Slack, or email.

Custom instructions

Add a personal prompt to any summary — "focus on the data", "explain like I'm 5", or anything else.

Multi-provider support

Configure keys for OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini. Switch between them per session without going to Settings.

Dark mode

Follows your system preference automatically, or lock it to light or dark in Settings. No eye strain at midnight.

Privacy

We have no servers.
We never see your data.

Keys stay on your device

API keys are stored only in chrome.storage.local — never in our servers, never in a database, never anywhere we can see.

Direct API calls only

When you summarize, page text goes directly from your browser to OpenAI / Anthropic / Google. It doesn't pass through us.

No tracking or analytics

No usage tracking. No telemetry. No crash reports phoning home. The extension is entirely self-contained.

Minimum permissions

activeTab only — no broad access to all sites. Content is extracted on demand, not by a persistent script.
